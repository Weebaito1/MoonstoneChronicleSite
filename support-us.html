I — A shared passion

Every great chronicle takes a little work.

The Moonstone Chronicle is a passion project built around our love of storytelling, roleplay, and the community we're creating together.

We want to provide an immersive, evolving world for everyone to enjoy, and a great deal of time, effort, and resources go into making that possible. If you'd like to help us along the way, optional Community Support Tiers are available for those who wish to contribute towards the ongoing running and development of the server.

Supporting us is never expected, and it will never be a requirement to enjoy everything The Moonstone Chronicle has to offer. Simply being part of our community, creating stories, and sharing this world with us is already an incredible contribution.

II — Reinvested in the world

Where does your support go?

Every contribution helps cover the costs of keeping the server running and gives us resources to continue improving and expanding the experience.

  • Server hosting and maintenance — keeping the world accessible.
  • Development and improvements — supporting new systems, scripts, and quality-of-life improvements.
  • World building and content — helping expand the world through environments, MLOs, assets, and other additions.
  • Community growth — giving us resources to keep building the experience we share.

We want to be completely transparent: 100% of contributions go towards The Moonstone Chronicle and its running costs. Not a single cent goes into anyone's personal pockets. This is a passion project, and any support we receive is reinvested into the community and the world we're building together.

III — Entirely optional

Community Support Tiers

We offer optional support tiers for those who would like to contribute. Whether it's a little or a lot, every contribution is genuinely appreciated and helps us continue doing what we love.

Support is entirely voluntary. All server content and roleplay experiences remain freely accessible regardless of whether someone chooses to contribute.

As a small token of appreciation, supporters may receive a dedicated Discord role and a personal thank-you from the team. There are no gameplay advantages, exclusive roleplay opportunities, or pay-to-win benefits attached to supporting the server.

We never want anyone to feel that they need to spend money to be part of our world. Your presence, creativity, and enthusiasm mean just as much to us as any financial contribution.

IV — A note on fan content

The Dark Pack Agreement

The Moonstone Chronicle is an independent, fan-created roleplay project inspired by the World of Darkness universe. We respect the intellectual property and creative work behind the setting, and any contributions accepted by the server are strictly in accordance with the Paradox Interactive Dark Pack Agreement.

All contributions are voluntary and intended solely to help cover the time, materials, and costs associated with maintaining and developing this community. We do not sell access to World of Darkness content, gameplay advantages, or exclusive experiences.

By supporting us, you're helping us continue to create and maintain a world everyone can enjoy, without placing a price tag on the stories we tell together.

V — From the team

Thank you for being part of our story.

Whether you've supported us financially, helped create the community, shared your ideas, or simply spent an evening creating stories with us, we want you to know how much it means.

The Moonstone Chronicle wouldn't be what it is without the people who bring it to life.
