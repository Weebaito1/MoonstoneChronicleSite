Keybinds & Commands

Keybinds &
Commands

Everything you need to know to find your way around.

Montana Falls has a variety of systems, interactions and features to discover, each with its own set of controls and commands. This page is intended to become your go-to reference for navigating them all.

Here, you'll eventually find a comprehensive collection of the server's in-game keybinds and available /commands, alongside brief explanations of what each one does and how to use it.

Whether you're looking for a particular interaction, trying to remember a command or simply familiarising yourself with the tools available to you, we'll aim to keep everything organised and easy to find.

A work in progress

Coming Soon™

We're still putting this guide together! As server features and systems are introduced, we'll continue adding their associated keybinds and commands, keeping the information updated as things evolve.

For now, there's nothing you need to memorise. We'll make sure the information is here when you need it.

Watch this space — your handy little reference guide is on its way!
