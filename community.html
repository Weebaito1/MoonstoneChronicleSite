Our Community

No Chronicle Is Written Alone.

A roleplay world is only as meaningful as the people who bring it to life. Behind every character is a storyteller, and behind every memorable moment is a group of people willing to create something together.

The Moonstone Chronicle is built around the belief that the best stories aren't written by one person alone. They're discovered through unexpected encounters, shared experiences, difficult decisions and the countless ways our characters influence one another. We're here to create a world where creativity is encouraged, stories are allowed to unfold naturally, and everyone has an opportunity to leave their mark.

The Heart of Our Community

Three principles. One shared experience.

Collaboration — A Story Is Better When Shared.

Roleplay is a conversation, not a performance. We believe in creating opportunities for one another, embracing unexpected directions and recognising that every participant has something to contribute.

Whether you're building a lasting friendship, developing a rivalry, creating a business or becoming part of a much larger story, we encourage players to think beyond their own characters and consider the experiences they can create together.

The most memorable stories are often the ones nobody could have planned alone.

Creativity — Rules Should Support the Story.

Our rules exist to provide structure, consistency and a fair environment for everyone, but they should never become unnecessary barriers to creativity.

We value intent, thoughtful interpretation and the spirit behind a player's actions. Not every situation can be anticipated or neatly accounted for in a rulebook, and we believe there's room for flexibility when it encourages meaningful roleplay without compromising the experience of others.

Bring us your ideas. Explore unusual character concepts. Find unexpected ways to interact with the world. We would rather encourage a little imagination than discourage a good story simply because it wasn't written in advance.

Respect — Every Storyteller Matters.

A shared world requires consideration for the people who inhabit it, both in and out of character.

We ask our community to respect one another's boundaries, contributions, time and enjoyment. Disagreements may happen, characters may clash, and stories may take unexpected turns, but the people behind those characters deserve courtesy and understanding.

We want everyone to feel comfortable contributing, asking questions, learning and finding their place within the community.

More Than a Character

The people behind the stories.

Although our shared world is built around roleplay, the community extends beyond the characters we create. Friendships, conversations, creative projects, shared laughter and the excitement of seeing someone's story unfold are all part of the experience.

You don't need to be an experienced roleplayer to find your place here. Whether you're a seasoned storyteller or stepping into the world of RedM roleplay for the first time, there's room to learn, experiment and grow alongside the community.

We encourage players to share ideas, support one another and take pride in the stories they're helping to create.

The People Behind the Chronicle

Guiding the world, supporting the storytellers.

Behind The Moonstone Chronicle is a team dedicated to maintaining the world, supporting its community and helping stories find room to flourish.

Our staff are here to assist with questions, provide guidance, review character submissions, resolve concerns and help facilitate the experiences that make the Chronicle what it is.

We believe staff should be approachable, fair and willing to listen. Rules and decisions should be guided by their purpose, not simply their wording, and we encourage players to reach out whenever they need assistance.

Ultimately, we're here to support the community, not dictate its stories.

Become Part of the Chronicle

The pages are open. The story is yours to help tell.

Montana Falls is a world in motion. Its history is still unfolding, its future uncertain, and the people who inhabit it will determine what comes next.

You might arrive with a story already in mind, or you might discover it along the way. You may change the course of someone's life, build something that outlasts your character or simply find a place where you enjoy spending your evenings.

Whatever brings you here, there's a place for you in the Chronicle.

No story is too small. No contribution goes unnoticed. And no chronicle is written alone.
