Getting Started

Your Story
Begins Here

Every Chronicle begins with a story. We'd love for yours to be among them.

Welcome to The Moonstone Chronicle, a World of Darkness-inspired roleplay experience set against the backdrop of the American frontier.

Whether you're entirely new to the World of Darkness, a long-time storyteller, or simply looking for somewhere to bring a new character to life, we're glad you've found your way here.

Getting started is simple. We've put together a few steps to help you find your footing, get acquainted with the world and prepare to make your mark on Montana Falls.

Your first steps

Find Your Footing

01

Discover the World

Before creating your character, take a little time to familiarise yourself with the setting.

The Chronicle is built upon the familiar foundations of the American frontier, with something far more unusual lurking beneath the surface. Humans, Kindred and those who hunt them all have their place in this world, though not everyone knows just how much exists beyond ordinary understanding.

Our World & Lore section offers an introduction to the setting, its history and the supernatural elements that shape it. Don't worry about knowing everything before you begin; there's always more to discover.

Explore World & Lore ⟶
02

Meet the Community

The heart of any Chronicle is the people who contribute to it.

Our Discord is where you'll find announcements, community discussions, support and opportunities to get involved. It's also the best place to introduce yourself, ask questions and get to know the people you'll soon be sharing stories with.

We encourage you to join in, have a chat and get comfortable. You don't need to have everything figured out before saying hello!

Join us on Discord ↗
03

Create Your Character

Once you've familiarised yourself with the setting, it's time to start thinking about who you'd like to bring into the world.

Whether you're interested in an ordinary person trying to make their way in the frontier, a Kindred navigating the politics of the night, or something else entirely, we're interested in characters with stories to tell and room to grow.

Take a look through our character and faction information to explore the possibilities. When you're ready, you can begin putting together your character with the character guide for submission and staff review.

We want to encourage creativity and interesting concepts, so if you have an idea that doesn't quite fit within the usual mould, don't be afraid to bring it to us. We're always happy to discuss possibilities.

Open the Character Guide ⟶
04

Find Your Place in Montana Falls

With your character approved and ready, it's time to step into the world.

Montana Falls is a developing community, with stories, relationships and opportunities waiting to take shape. You don't need an elaborate plan or a fully mapped-out character arc to get started. Sometimes the most interesting stories begin with a simple introduction, an unexpected encounter or a decision made along the way.

Take your time, meet people, explore the territory and allow your character's story to unfold naturally. The Montana Falls Guide will grow with the world.

Explore Montana Falls ⟶
A few final thoughts

A Few Things Before You Begin

We believe in Collaboration, Creativity and Respect. Our approach to roleplay is built around good intentions, thoughtful interactions and a shared commitment to collaborative storytelling.

You don't need to be an expert in the World of Darkness, know every piece of lore or arrive with years of roleplay experience. What matters most is a willingness to participate, create alongside others and contribute to a welcoming community.

If you have questions at any point, our staff team is happy to help. Reach out through Discord and we'll do our best to point you in the right direction. You can also read our community guidelines.

The world is waiting. Your Chronicle starts with a single step.
