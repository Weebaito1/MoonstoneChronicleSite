A world shaped by its players

Not a story already written.
A history waiting to happen.

Montana Falls is shaped by the people who choose to make it their home.

Its communities, politics, knowledge and opportunities will evolve through the actions of its inhabitants. A business can become a local institution. An alliance can alter the balance of power. A discovery can change how a community understands the world.

We provide the setting, the foundations and the opportunities. What becomes of them is shaped by the people who take part.

Your Story

Whether you arrive seeking fortune, a quiet life, political influence, forbidden knowledge or simply somewhere to belong, Montana Falls has room for stories of every kind.

You may never encounter the supernatural. You may find yourself drawn into its politics, help establish an entirely new order, or stand against it. There are no predetermined paths — only the world, its people, and the choices they make.
