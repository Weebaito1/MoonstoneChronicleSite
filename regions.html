The Regions of Montana Falls

Montana Falls is a vast and varied stretch of country, encompassing five territories, each with its own character, communities and way of life.

From the humid southern lowlands to the snowbound northern mountains, the land offers no shortage of places to settle, fortunes to pursue or stories waiting to unfold.

Some places are shaped by agriculture and industry, others by cattle, commerce or the unforgiving wilderness. Whether you seek the familiarity of a small town, the promise of a growing city or the solitude of the frontier, there is a place for you here.

The Five Territories

01 / The Southern Lowlands

Lemoyne

Old money · Agriculture · Southern tradition

A land of humid summers, sprawling plantations, fertile farmland and long-established wealth. Lemoyne is steeped in Southern tradition, where old families and established communities hold their ground amid a changing world. From the agricultural heart of Rhodes to the bustling streets of Saint Denis, the territory offers a striking contrast between country life and city ambition.

02 / The Heartlands

New Hanover

Cattle · Ranching · Industry

Open plains, rolling grasslands and hardworking communities define New Hanover. Cattle ranches, farms and growing settlements give the territory its character, with places like Valentine serving as gathering points for ranchers, traders and travellers. Life here is often rugged, practical and built on the strength of one's own hands.

03 / The Western Frontier

West Elizabeth

Forests · Settlements · Expansion

From the towering forests of Tall Trees to the open plains and prosperous settlements further south, West Elizabeth is a territory of contrasts. Established communities sit alongside vast stretches of wilderness, while towns such as Strawberry and Blackwater offer very different glimpses of frontier life. Opportunity is plentiful, but so is the land that remains untamed.

04 / The Arid Frontier

New Austin

Desert · Independence · Survival

A harsh and unforgiving country of desert expanses, rugged canyons and isolated settlements. New Austin demands resilience from those who call it home, where water and supplies can be precious and distances are often deceptive. From the dusty streets of Armadillo to the old frontier town of Tumbleweed, life is shaped by independence and the realities of survival.

05 / The Northern Wilderness

Ambarino

Mountains · Wilderness · Isolation

A vast and unforgiving wilderness of snow-covered peaks, frozen valleys and remote mountain country. Ambarino is among the least settled territories, where the elements are often more formidable than any human adversary. Those who venture into its high country must be prepared for isolation, harsh conditions and a way of life far removed from the comforts of civilisation.

A city on the rise

Saint Denis — The City of Opportunity

Among the many communities of Montana Falls, none is changing quite like Saint Denis.

A centre of commerce, industry and culture, the city is experiencing a period of remarkable growth, drawing newcomers from across the territories and beyond.

Fortunes are being made, businesses are taking root and new faces are finding their place among its crowded streets. For some, Saint Denis represents the promise of a better life. For others, it offers something far more valuable: opportunity, influence and a chance to begin anew.

It is here, amid the city's growing population and restless ambition, that the Kindred are beginning to gather.

The land is yours to explore

A world waiting for you

Montana Falls is more than its towns and territories. It is the ranches, homesteads, businesses, communities and quiet corners that give the land its character. Each place offers something different, and each has room for stories yet to be told.

Five territories. Countless possibilities. A world waiting for you.
