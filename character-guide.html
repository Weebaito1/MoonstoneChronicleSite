I — From idea to sheet

Build order

Start with the choices that make the character legible in a story. Let the numbers support that person.

  1. Concept, Nature, Demeanor, and ClanChoose a concept, Nature, and Demeanor, then select a clan. Give the character a want, a fear, and a reason their story begins here.
  2. Attributes: 7 / 5 / 3Prioritize Physical, Social, and Mental. Every Attribute starts at one dot; distribute 7 dots to the primary category, 5 to secondary, and 3 to tertiary. No Attribute can exceed five dots. See Saligia’s Attributes reference.
  3. Abilities: 13 / 9 / 5Prioritize Talents, Skills, and Knowledges, then distribute 13, 9, and 5 dots across those categories. No Ability can exceed three dots during this step. See Saligia’s Abilities reference.
  4. Advantages: 3 / 5 / 7Distribute 3 dots among Disciplines, 5 among Backgrounds, and 7 among Virtues. Virtues begin at one dot each; use Conscience or Conviction, Self-Control or Instinct, and Courage as appropriate to the character.
  5. Finishing touchesSet Humanity to Conscience + Self-Control, or an approved Path rating to Conviction + Instinct. Willpower begins equal to Courage. Record blood and Health traits using the V20 rules and the character’s Generation.
  6. Spend 15 freebie pointsUse the costs below to refine the character, including eligible Merits and Flaws. Then connect the sheet to the backstory and make sure the character has reasons to cooperate with others.

V20 freebie costs

Attributes: 5 per dot. Abilities: 2 per dot. Disciplines: 7 per dot. Backgrounds: 1 per dot. Virtues: 2 per dot. Humanity or Path: 1 per dot. Willpower: 1 per dot. Merits cost their listed value; Flaws can grant extra freebie points, up to 7 points from Flaws.

II — Reading the form

Common sheet fields

Sheet layouts differ, but these terms commonly appear in V20-based character creation.

Concept
A short description of the character’s role, history, or central tension.
Nature and Demeanor
Prompts for a deeper outlook and the face shown to others. They suggest roleplay, not a fixed script.
Attributes
Broad capabilities grouped as Physical, Social, and Mental.
Abilities
Learned or practiced capabilities, commonly grouped as Talents, Skills, and Knowledges.
Backgrounds
Connections, standing, resources, and other advantages a character has in the world.
Virtues
Traits used by many V20 sheets to represent a character’s moral and emotional foundations.
Humanity or Path
A moral framework for the character. Some vampires follow Humanity; others follow a distinct Path, if the chronicle allows it.
Willpower
A measure of resolve, often spent to push through pressure or resist influence under the game’s rules.
Blood and Generation
Vampire traits connected to lineage, hunger, and supernatural potential. Use the server’s definitions and calculations.
Health
A record of injury and condition. How it maps to roleplay or game effects is server-specific.
III — Advantages and complications

Merits

Merits represent optional advantages. Use only items approved by Chronicle staff, whether drawn from V20 or written for this setting; confirm the point value and any limits before adding one to a sheet. All sources are available for Merits and will be reviewed on a case by case basis. For wider V20 background, see Saligia’s Merit reference.

IV — Costs and complications

Flaws

Flaws add complications and vulnerabilities. Use only items approved by Chronicle staff, whether drawn from V20 or written for this setting; confirm the point value, limits, and portrayal with staff before adding one to a sheet. All sources are available for Flaws and will be reviewed on a case by case basis. For wider V20 background, see Saligia’s Flaw reference.

V — Supernatural gifts

Disciplines

Disciplines are a vampire’s supernatural abilities: they can sharpen the body and senses, influence other minds, or reach into stranger forces. The summaries below describe their general themes, not individual powers or rules. See the clan guide for traditional clan associations and Saligia’s V20 Discipline reference for wider context.

Animalism
Connects a vampire with animal instincts and the Beast within. Its powers can calm or provoke beasts, communicate with animals, and call or direct them.
Auspex
Heightens the senses and opens awareness to things ordinary perception misses, such as auras, hidden details, or distant supernatural impressions.
Celerity
Grants bursts of inhuman speed and reflexes, letting a vampire move, react, or act with startling quickness.
Chimerstry
Creates convincing illusions that deceive the senses, from a misleading sound or image to elaborate false experiences.
Dementation
Distorts perception and unsettles or intensifies emotions and mental states, often bringing a target’s fears or instability to the surface.
Dominate
Imposes a vampire’s will through supernatural commands, and at greater reaches can alter memories or reshape a person’s thoughts.
Fortitude
Hardens a vampire against injury and hardship, allowing them to endure damage that would incapacitate another.
Necromancy
Deals with the dead and the Underworld: sensing, contacting, summoning, or compelling spirits, depending on the path studied.
Obfuscate
Conceals a vampire from notice, helping them pass unseen, disguise their presence, or misdirect attention.
Obtenebration
Shapes supernatural darkness and shadow, using it to obscure places, reach into the world, or overwhelm those nearby.
Potence
Provides exceptional physical strength, turning ordinary feats of force into displays of monstrous power.
Presence
Magnifies a vampire’s personal magnetism, inspiring fascination, devotion, unease, or fear in those around them.
Protean
Transforms the vampire’s body, from growing predatory features to taking animal forms, and expresses an affinity with the wild.
Quietus
Uses blood with deadly precision, traditionally associated with silence, toxins, and weakening or destroying a foe.
Serpentis
Grants snake-like traits and transformations, along with powers tied to fascination, temptation, and the serpent’s predatory nature.
Thaumaturgy
Practices deliberate blood sorcery. Its distinct paths produce varied effects, while rituals provide more formal magical workings.
Vicissitude
Reshapes flesh and bone, allowing grotesque physical alterations to oneself or another.

Less-common and clan-specific disciplines

Some lineages are associated with specialized Disciplines that are rare or outside the usual choices. These examples are not an exhaustive list, and their inclusion here does not make them automatically available for characters; check with Chronicle staff before building around one.

Bardo
A spiritual practice associated with the Children of Osiris, focused on self-mastery and resisting the corrupting pull of the Beast.
Daimoinon
A feared Baali Discipline associated with infernal forces, probing hidden fears, and curses or corruption.
Koldunic Sorcery
An old Tzimisce tradition of blood magic that calls on the land and elemental forces such as earth, water, fire, and wind.
Melpominee
The Daughters of Cacophony’s voice-centered Discipline, turning song and speech into uncanny influence, far-reaching sound, or disorienting sonic effects.
Mytherceria
A Kiasyd-associated gift for uncanny knowledge and perception, revealing hidden truths and unsettling mysteries that others overlook.
Obeah
A Salubri Discipline associated with healing, spiritual perception, and protecting or restoring others.
Ogham
A rare, nature-bound form of blood sorcery associated with the Lhiannan, drawing on ancient symbols and the spirits of the land.
Sanguinus
A Blood Brothers Discipline that emphasizes the shared nature of the group, letting them pool or coordinate their bodies and senses.
Spiritus
An Ahrimanes-associated Discipline for dealing with spirits and channeling a connection to the spiritual world.
Temporis
A True Brujah Discipline that alters a vampire’s experience of time, granting effects such as heightened temporal perception or slowing events.
Valeren
A Salubri warrior tradition, sharpening supernatural perception and turning the vampire’s power toward confronting foes.
Visceratika
A Gargoyle Discipline that reflects their stony nature, granting resilience and an uncanny ability to merge with or disappear into stone.
VI — Before the story begins

Before you submit

  • Use the current server character sheet and approved creation rules.
  • Confirm clan, lineage, generation, sect, and supernatural options with staff.
  • Check all allocations, derived traits, Merits, and Flaws against the approved limits.
  • Make the sheet, backstory, and intended portrayal agree with one another.
  • Give the character a reason to cooperate, a meaningful limitation, and room to change.
  • Discuss boundaries and uncertain powers before they become a scene.
