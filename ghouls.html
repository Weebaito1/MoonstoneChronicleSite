A taste of Vitae

Mortal, but no longer untouched.

A ghoul is a mortal who has consumed vampire blood, known as Vitae. That blood can grant a mortal a measure of supernatural vitality, but it also creates a powerful dependency on the vampire whose blood they drink.

To an outsider, a ghoul may look like an ordinary person. Their connection to a Kindred patron can be hidden behind a job, a friendship, a debt, or a promise. But Vitae changes the relationship: it offers something extraordinary while making the ghoul increasingly vulnerable to the need for another taste.

The pull of addiction

Vitae is a need, not just a gift.

Vitae can be intoxicating and deeply habit-forming. A ghoul may crave the rush, comfort, or heightened sense of connection it brings, and the prospect of losing access to it can become frightening.

This dependence can shape a ghoul's choices, loyalties, and sense of what they can bear to lose. Each person experiences it differently, but the craving is a real force in their story—not simply a favor they can forget once the moment has passed.

Love, loyalty, and need

When devotion is tangled with dependence.

A ghoul's feelings for their patron can be complicated. Affection or loyalty may be sincere, but the euphoria and dependency associated with Vitae can make those feelings hard to separate from the need for another dose.

“You're not in love with your dealer, but with the drug they sell you.”

The comparison is a reminder to treat the bond as more than a simple romance: the ghoul may feel devotion, while also wrestling with addiction and an unequal balance of power. That does not make every emotion false, nor does it mean every ghoul has the same experience. It does mean the pull of Vitae deserves to be part of the story.
